Business websites.

Woviq builds business websites that explain your offer clearly and give customers a straightforward way to enquire. Based in Lahore, Pakistan, we combine responsive design, service content and technical SEO foundations. The result is a website your business can use as a clear starting point for conversations with customers.

The problem we help solve

Visitors should understand your business before they have to contact you. Unclear services, awkward mobile layouts and broken enquiry links create friction at the moment a customer wants to take action.

Who it’s for

Service businesses, professional firms and growing companies that need a credible website or a focused rebuild of an existing one.

What can be included

  • Structure and messagingA page plan based on what customers need to know before they enquire.
  • Responsive interfaceLayouts for phones, tablets and desktop screens with accessible navigation.
  • Search foundationsDescriptive metadata, crawlable service pages, a sitemap and suitable structured data.
  • Enquiry journeyClear contact options and a form connected to the agreed delivery channel.

How the work takes shape

We review your offer and existing content, agree the site structure, design representative pages, then build and check the full journey. Before handover, we review mobile layouts, content and the contact route with you.

Scope and practical limits

Copywriting, migration, hosting and ongoing content updates are scoped explicitly. Technical SEO provides foundations; it cannot guarantee rankings or enquiries.

Questions about business websites

Can you improve our existing website?

Yes. We first review its content, layout and hosting constraints. The proposal explains which parts can be retained and which need rebuilding.

Will it work on mobile?

Responsive layouts are part of the scope. We agree representative devices and check navigation, reading and enquiry flows at different screen sizes.

Is SEO included?

Technical foundations and service-page structure can be included. Keyword research, article production and ongoing search work are agreed separately.

Can we edit the website ourselves?

We can choose a suitable editing approach during discovery. A static site requires file changes; a content management system adds an editing interface and maintenance needs.
