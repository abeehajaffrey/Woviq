Business platforms.

Woviq plans business platforms that connect shared records and workflows across teams or branches. We start with how information moves, who can change it and where approvals are needed. The platform is then scoped around your operating model, with reporting and integrations designed to support day-to-day decisions rather than add unnecessary steps.

The problem we help solve

When each branch keeps its own version of information, staff can spend time reconciling records instead of serving customers. A shared platform needs clear ownership and access rules to remain useful.

Who it’s for

Multi-location businesses, operations teams and organisations that need a staff portal or a consistent process across departments.

What can be included

  • Operations mappingBranch responsibilities, shared data and exceptions documented before development.
  • Role-based workspacesRelevant views and actions for staff, managers and administrators.
  • Connected recordsAgreed rules for synchronisation, change history and conflict handling.
  • Management visibilityOperational summaries and exports built around defined business questions.

How the work takes shape

We compare the workflows used across locations, identify shared requirements and agree exceptions. A pilot workflow helps validate the model before broader rollout, training and support planning are finalised.

Scope and practical limits

Existing data quality, connectivity, vendor APIs and migration access affect feasibility. Multi-branch software does not automatically resolve inconsistent processes; operating responsibilities must be agreed alongside the build.

Questions about business platforms

Can branches have different permissions?

Yes. We can plan access by role and branch, including who can view, approve or change shared records. The permission model is reviewed before implementation.

Will it replace every tool we use?

Only where that makes sense. We assess whether existing tools should remain connected, be replaced or continue separately.

Can the platform work offline?

Offline use changes the data and synchronisation design. It needs to be stated as a requirement and assessed before scope and pricing are confirmed.

How do you handle rollout?

The rollout plan can begin with a single team or location. We agree migration, staff guidance and acceptance criteria before expanding access.
