POS systems.

Woviq scopes POS software around the way a business records sales, handles products and manages stock. We review checkout tasks, user permissions and branch requirements before choosing an implementation. Printer compatibility, offline behaviour and tax or payment integrations require specific assessment, so the proposal distinguishes confirmed functionality from items still being evaluated.

The problem we help solve

A checkout workflow needs to handle the practical details of a sale. Returns, discounts, permissions and stock adjustments can matter as much as the main billing screen.

Who it’s for

Retail shops and multi-branch businesses that need a tailored sales workflow or better connections between checkout, stock and reporting.

What can be included

  • Sales workflow designProducts, discounts, returns and receipt requirements mapped to daily operations.
  • Stock rulesAgreed handling for adjustments, branch transfers and product availability.
  • Staff accessDefined cashier, manager and administrator actions.
  • Compatibility reviewAssessment of required printers, scanners, payments and external systems.

How the work takes shape

We observe the required sale and return journeys, catalogue hardware and review integration documentation. After defining acceptance cases, we build the agreed workflow and test representative transactions before discussing rollout.

Scope and practical limits

Hardware, payment certification, fiscal requirements and tax integrations are not assumed. Offline operation, data migration and accounting connections must be assessed and quoted explicitly.

Questions about pos systems

Will it work with our printer and barcode scanner?

We need the exact models and connection methods to assess compatibility. Hardware support is confirmed through agreed checks rather than assumed from a brand name.

Can it handle more than one branch?

Multi-branch use can be scoped with separate stock, staff permissions and shared reporting. Synchronisation and connectivity requirements are reviewed first.

Does it support tax integrations?

Any required fiscal or tax integration needs a specific review of the authority requirements, provider interfaces and business eligibility. No compliance status is assumed.

Can staff sell when the internet is down?

Offline sales require local storage, recovery and synchronisation rules. Tell us this is essential during discovery so it can be assessed before choosing the architecture.
