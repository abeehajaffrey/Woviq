WhatsApp & web chatbots.

Woviq designs WhatsApp and website chatbots that help customers find information and send useful enquiries. We agree what the assistant may answer, which sources it can use and when a person should take over. The build can combine structured conversation flows with AI responses, depending on the task and the risks involved.

The problem we help solve

Repeated questions can interrupt your team, while unclear automated replies can frustrate customers. A useful chatbot needs a defined purpose, maintained information and a clear route to human help.

Who it’s for

Businesses with repeated product or service questions, a need for structured enquiry capture, or a support team that wants to organise common conversations.

What can be included

  • Conversation planningA defined set of customer intents, answers and escalation rules.
  • Knowledge preparationApproved sources and boundaries for what the assistant can say.
  • Enquiry routingAgreed fields, destinations and handoff to the relevant team.
  • Response checksSample questions, failure scenarios and review of uncertain answers.

How the work takes shape

We review common conversations, define answer boundaries and choose the channel. A prototype is checked against representative questions before integrations, handoff behaviour and monitoring responsibilities are finalised.

Scope and practical limits

AI can give incorrect answers. We plan limits and human escalation, but cannot promise perfect responses. WhatsApp Business Platform access, templates, messaging and AI usage may involve separate provider costs and approvals.

Questions about whatsapp & web chatbots

Can the chatbot answer from our documents?

It can be designed to use approved source material. We review document quality, access and update ownership, then test whether the selected approach answers the agreed questions well enough.

Can it hand over to a person?

Human handoff is an explicit part of conversation design. The exact route depends on your WhatsApp or support platform and how your team handles incoming messages.

Is this an official WhatsApp partnership?

No partnership is claimed. WhatsApp Business is a tool we may build with, and its platform rules and provider requirements still apply.

Can it guarantee correct answers?

No. AI responses can be wrong. Restricted topics, source checks, fallback messages and escalation can help manage that limitation.
