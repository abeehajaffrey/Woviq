Ecommerce & online ordering.

Woviq builds ecommerce and online ordering experiences around your products and fulfilment process. We help businesses choose between Shopify and a custom approach, then plan the catalogue, checkout and order workflow. Payment providers, delivery rules and inventory connections are assessed before implementation so the scope reflects how your business operates.

The problem we help solve

A storefront is only one part of selling online. Inconsistent product information, unsuitable delivery rules and disconnected order handling can create work for your team after every purchase.

Who it’s for

Retailers, product brands and food or service businesses that need an online catalogue, checkout or structured ordering process.

What can be included

  • Catalogue experienceProduct organisation, useful filters and clear product information.
  • Checkout planningPayment methods and checkout behaviour matched to provider availability.
  • Order operationsAgreed delivery, collection, notification and fulfilment flows.
  • Integration assessmentA review of inventory, POS and other systems that must exchange order data.

How the work takes shape

We map the product range, customer journey and order fulfilment process. After comparing platform options, we implement the agreed storefront, test sample orders and review operational handover with the people handling fulfilment.

Scope and practical limits

Shopify plans, paid themes, payment charges and extensions are separate costs. Provider eligibility, settlement and regional availability must be verified for the business. Sales are not guaranteed.

Questions about ecommerce & online ordering

Should we use Shopify or custom commerce?

Shopify can suit businesses that want established store administration. A custom build may fit unusual ordering rules. We compare requirements, recurring costs and ownership before recommending a route.

Can you connect our POS to online orders?

Possibly. We need the POS vendor, available APIs, stock rules and branch setup before confirming a reliable integration.

Are payment charges included?

No. Payment-provider fees and platform subscriptions are billed under their own terms. We separate these from implementation in the quote.

Can you move an existing store?

Migration can be scoped after reviewing product data, customers, orders, URLs and platform export options. Sensitive customer data needs an agreed handling process.
