What does Woviq do?

Woviq is a digital solutions company based in Lahore, Pakistan. We build business websites, ecommerce experiences, custom software, dashboards, POS systems, chatbots and workflow automations.

Where should my business start?

Start with the problem that creates the most friction for customers or staff. Tell us what is happening today and what should improve. We can then discuss a focused scope before recommending a tool.

How much does a project cost?

We quote after understanding the scope. The model is a one-time implementation fee plus optional monthly support. Domains, hosting, software subscriptions and third-party usage are separate and identified during scoping.

Can you improve an existing website or system?

Yes. We review the existing setup, available access and technical constraints before proposing changes. Where a system cannot be extended reliably, we explain the options before any rebuild is agreed.

Do you guarantee search rankings or AI answers?

No. We build search foundations and useful, accessible content. Search rankings, AI-search inclusion, sales and perfect chatbot answers cannot be guaranteed.
