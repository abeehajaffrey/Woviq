Privacy, clearly.

How this website handles enquiries and information.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Who to contact

Woviq is based in Lahore, Pakistan. For privacy questions or a request about an enquiry, email hello@woviqai.com.

The enquiry builder

The builder runs in your browser. It uses your name, contact details, service selection, project description and any optional business or budget information to prepare a message. It does not send those fields to a Woviq form server. Opening WhatsApp places that information in a WhatsApp draft; the URL containing the draft may be processed by WhatsApp and appear in browser history. You must press Send in WhatsApp for us to receive the message.

Please do not include passwords, payment details, medical records or other sensitive information. If you leave the page, the website does not save the form in local storage. Your browser may retain entries through its own features.

When you contact us

If you send us a WhatsApp message or email, we receive the information included in that message and use it to discuss your project, respond to your questions and manage the resulting business conversation. We may need to retain relevant correspondence for project delivery, business records or legal requirements. Ask us about access, correction or deletion by email; applicable obligations may limit what can be removed.

Hosting and third-party services

This website is designed for GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical request information such as IP addresses and browser details under their own terms. WhatsApp and your email provider handle information when you use their services. Their privacy policies apply to those interactions.

Cookies and analytics

This version contains no advertising scripts, analytics SDKs, embedded chat service or third-party font requests. It does not set cookies or use local storage. Basic interaction hooks are present for a future analytics integration but do not transmit information. This notice must be updated before analytics or other data services are added.

Changes to this notice

If the website’s data handling changes, this page should be updated and its date revised. Contact us if you need clarification before sharing project information.
